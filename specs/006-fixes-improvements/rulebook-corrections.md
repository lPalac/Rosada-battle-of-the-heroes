# Rulebook corrections from feature 006

These are the rules you decided on 2026-11-11 while we fixed the playtest issues. The game already plays them. Each line below is a draft sentence for `Basic Rules 2_5 - corrections.docx` (or for `Hero Rules 2_0.docx` where noted). Reword them as you like; the game follows the decision, not this wording.

| # | Decision | Draft sentence for the rulebook | Spec |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Destiny for a new check | "Whenever a unit newly puts the enemy King in check, by any means (a Lead, an ability, a promotion, or a line opened by a move), the checking side gains 2 Destiny. A side gains this at most once per turn." | Q-61, Q-73, Q-74, FR-001 |
| 2 | Checking your own King | "If a side uncovers a check on its own King, the other side gains the 2 Destiny." | Q-74, FR-001 |
| 3 | Only new checks count | "A check that already existed gives nothing. A unit that joins an existing check is a new check." | Q-73 |
| 4 | Destiny symbols on Starting abilities (Hero Rules) | "Each Destiny symbol on a Starting hero sheet is one use of that ability and costs 1 Destiny. *Warhorse*, *Rejuvenating Stroll*, *Elusive* and *Future Sight* are free." | Q-62, FR-002 |
| 5 | Both uses at once (Hero Rules) | "*Preaching* may be used once per turn. When both uses are left, you may spend one use or both uses in that one use; spending both pays both costs and gives the stronger effect." | Q-62a, Q-78, FR-017 |
| 6 | Inspired Lead | "*Inspired Lead* may be paid at any time before the Lead die is rolled, also after the Lead is announced. It is still once per turn." | Q-65, FR-007 |
| 7 | Leads and your own King | "A Lead that would leave your own King in check is not allowed. This also applies to an ability that Leads a unit instead of the Lead." | FR-014 |
| 8 | Must not check the King | "No ability may make a unit it creates, moves, places or changes check either King, whether or not the ability shows the 'Must not check the King' symbol. A discovered check (a line opened by the ability) is allowed." | Q-76, FR-016 |
| 9 | The path of a Move (Hero Rules) | "The square the hero starts its Move on counts as part of its path." | Q-63, FR-003 |
| 10 | Mirror picks | "In a Mirror game the two heroes of a side are different: Light picks one hero for both sides, then Dark picks a different hero for both sides." | Q-79, FR-013 |
| 11 | Mirror Epic destinies | "In a Mirror game each Epic destiny is chosen once for a class: the first hero in the choice order chooses for its class and both heroes of that class take it; then the other side's hero of the other class chooses." | Q-64, Q-75, FR-012 |
| 12 | Sandbox and checkmate (digital game only) | "When rules are turned back on after free play, the turn goes on where it stopped. If a King is checkmated in that position, the players are told and may continue the game or go back to free play; the checkmate is ruled when the next turn begins." | Q-66, FR-006, FR-009 |

Rule 12 is about the digital game only. It does not belong in the printed rulebook unless you want to describe the app.
